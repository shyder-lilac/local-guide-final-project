Shopping in Seattle

Unique Shops

Easy Street Records isn't just a record store—it's a community hub for music lovers. It's got a great vibe, the staff are experts, and the selection of vinyl, CDs, and music equipment is well thought out. It's the perfect place to discover new artists or find that special album you've been searching for.

Prism is a creative's dream in Ballard, with a curated selection of art supplies, unique gifts, and design tools. Whether you're an artist, crafter, or just love beautiful stationery, Prism's vibrant space and expert staff make it a must-visit for anyone looking to add a touch of creativity to their life.

Local Markets

Pike Place Market is Seattle's famous, historic market. It's a busy place with vendors selling fresh produce, artisan crafts, flowers, and famous treats like the original Pike Place Chowder and Daily Dozen Donuts. You'll definitely want to check out the flying fish at the fishmonger's and the first Starbucks store in the world. The market also has street performers, antique shops, and hidden gems like the Gum Wall and Rachel the Pig, the market's beloved bronze piggy bank. And don't forget to rub her nose for good luck!

Fremont's Sunday Market is a bustling outdoor market, also known as the "Center of the Universe." It's a great place to find vintage items, handmade crafts, antiques, and local art. It's a great place to hunt for unique souvenirs, quirky gifts, and even street food. The market's got a real eclectic vibe, which reflects Fremont's reputation as Seattle's most artistic and offbeat neighborhood.

No matter where you go, you'll find something special to buy as a souvenir in Seattle.
